## Channel
- Format: 30-45 second vertical Shorts, fast "brain rot" pacing, word-by-word pop captions.
- Niche: weird history, strange science and true stories that sound made up.
- Audience: scrolling fast; the first two seconds decide everything.

## Preferences
- Facts over hype. Never invent numbers or quotes.
- Titles are curiosity gaps, not spoilers.
