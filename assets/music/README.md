# Background music

Drop royalty-free tracks here (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.ogg`). Each video picks one at random,
loops it under the narration at a low volume (`CONTENT_PILOT_MUSIC_VOLUME`, default 0.10) and
fades it out at the end. No tracks, no music.

Only use music you are licensed to publish on YouTube, for example the YouTube Audio Library
(studio.youtube.com > Audio Library, filter by "no attribution required"). You can commit tracks
here, or keep them out of git and set the `MUSIC_URL` secret to a direct link to a .zip of them.
